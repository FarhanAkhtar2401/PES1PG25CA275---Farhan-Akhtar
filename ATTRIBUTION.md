# Data attribution

ATMOSPHERE uses weather forecast, air-quality forecast and geocoding data from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/).

Weather and air-quality values are model-based estimates and forecasts, not guaranteed direct observations from a nearby weather station or regulatory monitor. The dashboard shows US AQI and European AQI as separate scales; these are not India's CPCB AQI. Review the provider's current terms and attribution requirements before public use.
